Privacy.

How Rukki Bot relates to your server's data — explained plainly.

What Rukki Bot Works With

Rukki Bot works within the permissions and configuration it has been granted in your Discord server. To provide its features, it uses the following:

  • Partnership Maker — your partnership configuration, schedules, partner queue, and usage records needed to run automation and track allowances.
  • AI Analyzer — available historical server activity, event information, and conversations in your server, used to identify patterns and generate contextual recommendations.

Your Control

  • What Rukki Bot can access is controlled through standard Discord role and channel permissions in your server.
  • Features such as AI Analyzer depend on your current Rukki Bot configuration and can be adjusted by server management.
  • Recommendations are generated to support management decisions; they do not change anything in your server on their own.

AI Analyzer Recommendations

AI Analyzer generates recommendations from patterns found in your server's history. Recommendations are intended as decision support for server management — not as guarantees about future activity.

Questions About Your Data

For any privacy-related question or request regarding Rukki Bot, contact us via the support page.
