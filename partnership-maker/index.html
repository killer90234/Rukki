Recommendations, Backed by Your Server's History

AI Analyzer uses available historical server activity, event information, and conversations to generate contextual recommendations. Recommendations are intended to support server management decisions, not replace them.

Grounded in Your Server's Own Data

Every recommendation refers back to patterns from previous activity, events, and conversations in your community — not generic advice.

Permission-Based Access

AI Analyzer works within the permissions and configuration Rukki Bot has in your Discord server. You stay in control of what it can access through standard server settings.

Decision Support, Not Autopilot

The analyzer suggests what could be done next. Server management always decides which actions to take — nothing changes in your community without you.

Transparent Reasoning

Each recommendation explains the historical pattern behind it, so your team can judge whether it fits your community before acting on it.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Partnership Maker?

Partnership Maker is a Rukki Bot feature that automates Discord server partnerships. You configure your preferences once, set a schedule, and Rukki manages the repetitive parts of running partnership activity while tracking your usage.

How does automatic partnership management work?

After you add Rukki Bot and configure your partnership rules, you choose when the workflow should run — for example daily at 8:30 PM. At the scheduled time, Rukki processes your partner queue according to your settings and records the results so you can review usage at any time.

How many free partnerships does every server receive?

Every Discord server receives 5 free partnerships each month.

What is AI Analyzer?

AI Analyzer examines historical server activity, events, and conversations to identify patterns and provide contextual recommendations for server management.

Which plans include AI Analyzer?

AI Analyzer is included with the Pro and Max plans. It is not part of the free allowance or the Basic plan — Free and Basic focus on Partnership Maker.

Does AI Analyzer guarantee that activity will increase?

No. It provides recommendations based on historical patterns and available server data. Server management decides which actions to take.

Can AI Analyzer tell me what to do next?

It can suggest practical next steps based on patterns found in previous server activity, events, and conversations.

Can I schedule partnership activity?

Yes. Scheduling is a core part of Partnership Maker — pick the days and times that suit your community, such as daily at 8:30 PM, and automation runs accordingly.

What happens when I reach my partnership limit?

Automation pauses for the rest of the cycle once your plan's allowance is used up. Usage tracking shows exactly where you stand, and everything resumes automatically when your allowance resets.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes. You can move between Basic, Pro, and Max at any time as your community grows. Each tier simply increases how many partnerships your server can run per cycle.
