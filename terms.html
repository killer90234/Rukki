Using Rukki Bot

By inviting Rukki Bot to your Discord server, you agree to use it in line with Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, and with these terms.

Plans and Allowances

Every Discord server receives a free allowance of 5 partnerships per month through Partnership Maker. Paid plans (Basic, Pro, Max) increase this monthly allowance. Plan limits and features are described on the pricing page. AI Analyzer availability depends on the current Rukki Bot configuration.

Acceptable Use

  • Do not use Rukki Bot to violate Discord's rules or any applicable law.
  • Do not attempt to disrupt, overload, or reverse-engineer the service.
  • You are responsible for how you configure automation in your community, including who has access to those settings.

Acknowledgements About AI Analyzer

AI Analyzer provides recommendations based on patterns found in historical server activity, events, and conversations. Recommendations are intended to support server management decisions — they are not guarantees about future activity, and server management remains responsible for decisions made.

Changes to the Service

We may update, change, or discontinue parts of the service over time. Material changes that affect paid plans will be communicated to plan holders.

Questions

If you have questions about these terms, contact us via the support page.
