Getting Started with Rukki Bot.

Everything you need to set up Partnership Maker and make sense of AI Analyzer recommendations.

1. Connect Rukki Bot

Invite Rukki Bot to your Discord server using the Invite Rukki Bot button. You will need the Manage Server permission in the server you want to add it to.

During authorization, Discord will show you which permissions Rukki Bot requests. You can review and adjust what the bot can access at any time through your server's role and channel settings — Rukki Bot only works within the permissions it has been granted.

2. Set Up Partnership Maker

  • Configure — set your partnership preferences and rules for the server. Each server is configured independently.
  • Schedule — choose when the workflow should run, for example daily at 8:30 PM.
  • Automate — Rukki processes your partner queue according to your settings and records the results.
  • Track usage — monitor how many partnerships have been used against your plan allowance from the dashboard.

Every server starts with a free allowance of 5 partnerships per month. When you reach your limit, automation pauses until the next cycle or until you upgrade your plan.

3. Understand AI Analyzer

AI Analyzer reviews historical server activity, events, and conversations available to Rukki Bot and turns them into contextual recommendations — for example, suggesting a community event when chat activity has decreased compared with previous active periods.

A few things worth knowing:

  • Recommendations are based on patterns found in your server's history, not guarantees about future activity.
  • Each recommendation explains the reasoning behind it so your team can judge whether it fits your community.
  • AI Analyzer supports management decisions — it does not make changes to your server on its own.
  • Availability depends on your current Rukki Bot configuration.

4. Managing Plans and Limits

You can compare plan allowances on the pricing page. Plans can be changed as your community grows — each tier increases how many partnerships your server can run per cycle. Usage tracking always shows exactly where you stand.
